Consumer Lending Portfolio: Growth, Risk & Return

Lending Club, 2007–2018 · interactive results · hover any chart for details · code & full report on GitHub

1. Growth & approval funnel

Volume grew ~400× from 2008 to 2018 while the approval rate fell from 15.1% (2013) to 5.0%. Applications tripled from 2015 to 2018; approvals rose only 18%.

2007 covers June–December only. Approval rate = approved ÷ (approved + rejected applications).

2. The 2016 slowdown

Funded volume was down year-over-year for 10 straight months (Jul-2016 → Apr-2017). April 2016's −43% month-over-month drop is a base effect from the March spike; year-over-year it was −0.4%.
What drove it? H2-2016 vs. H2-2015

Funded volume = applications × approval rate × average loan size. Demand rose; the drop came from approvals.

Did applicants get worse? Mix vs. rate

Approved loans use FICO at application; declined applications use Risk_Score (assumed comparable). Below 660 nobody is approved (knockout rule); ~80% of declined applications have no score because they fail eligibility checks before a credit pull.

3. Risk vs. return by grade

Grade rank-orders risk well (5.4% → 42.0% bad rate), and every grade is profitable, but realized return peaks in grade B (8.3%) and falls to 2.2% in G. Lower grades are not paid for their extra risk.

2014–2015 issued, 36-month loans (all matured). Realized return = total collected ÷ total funded − 1, 3-year total, before fees and funding costs.

4. Application-time risk drivers

Every variable carries signal, but none comes close to grade: single variables separate risk by 1.5–1.9×, versus ~7.8× for grade. Income's Information Value is 0.06 (weak), with a monotonic WOE.
